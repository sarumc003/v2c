# Village 2 City Design Brief

- Design read: A public-interest platform for donors and communities, balancing warmth, dignity, trust, and practical action.
- Concept spine: The giving pathway. One small, concrete contribution travels from a community need to a verified handover, then becomes a visible proof of care.
- Delivery tier: cinema.
- Animation mode: animated-website (user explicitly requested cinematic animated scrolling).
- Journey shape: single-shot. The supplied logo artwork is used as the brand anchor while a restrained motion treatment introduces the chapters.
- Journey: 1) Community begins with a need; 2) a donor chooses a useful item; 3) the item moves toward a recipient; 4) delivery evidence supports transparency; 5) the community can see approved progress.
- World grammar: white and forest-green humanitarian identity with emerald accents and restrained muted gold, clean editorial composition, natural light, tactile paper and education-item motifs, no fake statistics or fabricated testimonials.
- Mobile framing: central focal area and readable chapter copy, navigation collapses, animations simplify for reduced motion.
- Locked palette: forest green #176B45, emerald #168A5B, white #FFFFFF, warm white #F7FAF8, muted gold #C8A94D, ink #16372A, gray #52635B. The green/white/gold palette is inherited from the supplied logo.
- Locked type: Outfit display + Inter body for approachable clarity.
- Section plan: cinematic journey, community needs editorial list, how-it-works numbered path, transparency split section, community updates feed, clear final action.
- Asset plan: use supplied logo unchanged; use generated/encoded logo-led motion poster only as a temporary fallback because image/video generation is unavailable on the connected media account; build favicon from a simplified V2C mark; generate no replacement logo.
- CTA inventory: Explore Community Needs (primary), Become a Donor (secondary), View All Needs (feed), Share a Need (authenticated request flow), Sign In (account access).
- Truth rule: show no invented achievements, beneficiary counts, donation totals, verified claims, or testimonials. Empty database states must be explicit.
